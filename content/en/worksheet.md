# Governance Analysis Worksheet

[Back to home](README.md)

Choose a specific task and identify what the relevant responsibility must cover. Clear participants, objects, conditions, and evidence matter more than a list of mechanism names. Motivating cases belong directly in the explanation of why a responsibility is needed on its page. This worksheet supports analysis rather than providing a separate case study.

## Task and responsibility

| Question | What to record |
|---|---|
| What is the task? | Goal, deliverables, mandatory conditions, and deadline |
| Which responsibility is being analyzed? | The governance function in focus; do not expand every issue at once |
| Why is it needed? | The concrete failure path without that function, its effects, and why other responsibilities do not cover it |
| Where do the requirements come from? | The mandate, organizational rules, information-use conditions, and who may revise them |
| What is permitted? | Participants, information, tools, operational scope, resource budget, and authorization validity period |
| Who is responsible? | Who judges, executes, and reviews, and which functions existing programs already perform |

## Execution and evidence

| Question | What to record |
|---|---|
| What is visible? | Proposals, calls, returns, external state, progress, and unobservable parts |
| At which step does failure occur? | A concrete error, omission, stall, or unknown state in an execution |
| How should the system respond? | Response conditions, time limits, executable actions, and takeover paths |
| What authority is needed for the response? | Current permissions for retries, corrections, changes, pauses, or handoffs |
| What happens while waiting? | What may continue, what must pause, and who remains responsible |
| How is the outcome confirmed? | Checks of actual state, affected scope, necessary conditions, and unknown parts |
| What is retained? | Links among requirements, approvals, actions, effects, anomalies, verification, and responsibility |
| How are records protected? | Conditions for access, retention, deletion, and minimization of sensitive information |

## Checks before writing a responsibility page

Can one case explain the responsibility? If it requires a growing collection of exceptions, roles, and systems to work, first choose a clearer failure path.

If the draft says only “monitoring agent,” “human confirmation,” “logging,” or “automatic recovery,” explain what it actually observes, decides, changes, or establishes. When using a success message, state the scope of the conclusion it supports. When claiming a responsibility is necessary, also identify which existing arrangements already perform the required functions.
